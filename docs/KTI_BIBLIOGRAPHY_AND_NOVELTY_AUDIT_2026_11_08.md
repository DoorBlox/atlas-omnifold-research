## 1. Clear conclusion from prior work

**What is already established and NOT novel:**
- [ATLAS 2024 original PRL](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.261803) publishes an unbinned **24-dimensional simultaneous** Z+jets measurement. The two relevant jet variables are among the 24 released observables; their joint information is already contained in the ATLAS measurement. The original publication's Figure 2 also demonstrates **constructed observables** including tau21, dilepton–jet angular separation and mean leading-jet mass versus leading-jet pT. We cannot claim to invent multidimensional jet measurements, derived observables, or post hoc binnings.
- [ATLAS 2024 boosted top jet study](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.109.112016) explicitly publishes **one- and two-dimensional differential charged-jet-substructure measurements** (different physics process and jet definition). ATLAS has performed 2D charged-substructure measurements before this project.
- [ATLAS 2025 jet track functions](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2025.139680) uses machine-learning unfolding for track-sensitive jet physics (different dataset/process). The [2026 practical unfolding guide](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-15265-9) discusses released multidimensional measurements and custom downstream 1D projections. Neither general task is a novelty claim.
- [Arratia et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-0221/17/01/P01024) sets out publishing and reusing unbinned measurements; [Andreassen et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.124.182001) introduced OmniFold. Both are prior methodology, not inventions of this KTI.
- [Desai, Long & Nachman 2025](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-14835-1) directly studies event correlations induced by unfolding. We cannot imply our uncertainty warnings are a new general discovery.

**What was NOT identified in this bounded search:** a separate indexed peer-reviewed article that reports this **specific 10.594606399536133 GeV × 11-track, pT_trackj1 >5 GeV, four-cell mass–multiplicity projection** of the **2024 ATLAS Zenodo release** with these **particular signed source-correlation sensitivity comparisons**. Searches reviewed ATLAS/CERN original paper figure captions, the 2026 practical guide's published-research table, and targeted title/abstract keyword searches for the exact observable pair and OmniFold release. **This is absence of a located match, NOT proof of global publication priority.** An independent INSPIRE-HEP / Scopus forward-citation and conference/proceedings search remains prudent before submission.

**Defensible distinctive contribution:** a **fully reproducible, independently audited educational/secondary-analysis case study** applying a frozen two-observable four-cell projection to the released ATLAS 24D weighted measurement, showing two marginals plus a separate association coordinate, using correctly distinguished shared-generator versus independent-pseudotruth reference covariance and source-level **conditional** uncertainty sensitivity with transparent limits. This is **not** new ATLAS measurement data, new theory, validated generator exclusion, a first-ever 2D jet measurement, or an ATLAS-approved 2D systematic uncertainty method. At the current level its primary value is **school KTI / computational reproducibility**, not established new specialist-journal physics.
